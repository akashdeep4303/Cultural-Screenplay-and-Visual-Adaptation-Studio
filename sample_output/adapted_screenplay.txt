CHITthi JO NAHI KHOLI
Majhi adaptation — rural Majha — Amritsar / Tarn Taran belt, Punjab
Output script: roman

SCENE 1
EXT. KOTHI DA AANGAN - EARLY MORNING

Brick kothi with a small aangan, iron gate that never latches, peepal shade at the wall, charpai against the verandah

A modest do-manzila kothi. Dew on the grass. A lohe da gate that never quite closes. Jaswant (28), restless, stands with an unopened envelope. He has not slept.
Bebe (55) watches from the verandah de darwaze, a phulkari-border dupatta around her shoulders. Simran (22) sits on the aangan di kandh, pretending to check her mobile.
He turns the envelope over. The handwriting is his Mama's.
Jaswant does not answer. He puts the letter back in his khaddar jacket de pocket and walks toward the rasoi door. Bebe's worry stays on her face. Simran follows him with her eyes, then looks down.

                    SURJIT KAUR
          Tu late aa gya, puttar. Jadon kujh gal hundi ae, tu
          aidaan hi ghumda ae.

                    JASWANT SINGH
          Kujh gal nahi, Bebe. Saah lain nu bahar aaya si.

                    SIMRAN KAUR
          Oh chiTThi do din to pocket vich pae. Ja khol, ja
          Bebe nu de de.

                    SURJIT KAUR
          Je mere veer di chiTThi ae te eh ghar di gal ae.
          Ghar di gal merethon lukai nahi jaandi.


SCENE 2
INT. RASOI - MORNING

Smoke-darkened rasoi with a gas stove, steel glasses, aluminium kettle, and a wooden almirah of masala tins

Steam from a aluminium kettle. Three steel glasses wait on the table. The rasoi is the true centre of the house. Harpreet (27) has arrived early, as he always does when Jaswant is in trouble. He leans against the counter, trying to keep the mood light.
Silence. Jaswant's hand stays on the pocket that holds the letter. The aluminium kettle clicks off.
Jaswant sits. He does not take the letter out. Simran places a cup in front of him, then one in front of Bebe. Harpreet looks at the floor, suddenly not funny.
Jaswant stands, still carrying the letter, and leaves through the back door. Simran starts to speak, then stops.

                    HARPREET SINGH
          Aidaan lagda ae jaan shart haar ke rasta vi haar
          aaya hovein, yaar.

                    JASWANT SINGH
          Mama Jagtar ne likheya ae. Ohnu shop chahidi ae mere
          kolon. Bebe hun vi samajhdi ae main padhda peaa
          haan.

                    SIMRAN KAUR
          Tainu ohnau dassiya si ikk saal baaki ae.

                    SURJIT KAUR
          Main sunn rahi haan. Apni rasoi vich main koi parai
          nahi.

                    SURJIT KAUR
          Beh ja. Chai pee le. Phir sach dass, oh gal nahi jo
          tu bazaar vaaste tyaar karke rakhi ae.

                    JASWANT SINGH
          Kam ton baad, Bebe. Main dasanga kam ton baad.

                    SURJIT KAUR
          Kam ton baad di gal naal jhooth buddha ho janda ae.


SCENE 3
EXT. PIND DA CHOWK / MANDI ROAD - AFTERNOON

Open mandi road with kiryana, cycle-repair, and a reri of kulche-chole; buses throw dust

A busy street of small shops, buses, and heat. Jaswant walks with Harpreet. The letter is still in his pocket. A hawker calls out. A bicycle bell cuts through the noise.
They stop near a kulche-chole di reri. Jaswant almost opens the envelope, then puts it away again.
He keeps the letter. They part. The pind da chowk continues without noticing.

                    HARPREET SINGH
          Je shop le li, tu pind vich rehna. Te je rehana, oh
          bande banjna jehda Bebe ne paise nu pehlan hi dass
          chhaddiya ae.

                    JASWANT SINGH
          Je na kar ditta, Bebe kahegi main Mama de kam ton
          sharmaunda haan.

                    HARPREET SINGH
          Phir gall gali vich na kar. Ghar ja, Bebe tere moonh
          te vece je tu gall karein.

                    HARPREET SINGH
          Simran jaandi ae. Bebe lagbhag jaandi ae. Naatak
          sirf tu karan peaa ae.

                    JASWANT SINGH
          Shaam ton pehlan ghar jaanga. Kise paraye di himmat
          chukk ke nahi avanga.


SCENE 4
INT. BAITHAK - EVENING

Baithak with one sofa, floor dari, framed family photograph, and a low table for the letter

The house is quieter. A single lamp. Bebe sits in the Bebe di kursi. Simran stands near the window. Jaswant enters, dusty from the street, the same khaddar jacket, the same letter.
He takes the envelope out and places it on the chhoti mez between them. He does not sit until Bebe nods. He waits for Bebe's nod before taking a seat — baithak etiquette.
Bebe picks up the letter, feels its weight, and does not open it yet. She looks at her son, then at her daughter.
Simran sits. Jaswant sits. The letter stays on the table, no longer hidden, not yet finished.

                    JASWANT SINGH
          Mama ne course chhadd ke kiryana sambhalan nu keha
          ae. ChiTThi is vaaste lukai kyunki mainu darr si
          tuhanu niiraan na kar deaan.

                    SURJIT KAUR
          Niiraan te main seh laini aan, puttar. Par ghar vich
          aakhri jaanan vaali nahi ban sakdi.

                    SIMRAN KAUR
          Assi tere naal behnde. Eh kothi da matlab hi eh ae.

                    JASWANT SINGH
          Maaf kar de, Bebe. Shop baare hun vi pakka nahi.
          Pakka eh ae ke pehlan dassna chahida si.

                    SURJIT KAUR
          Phir assaN ikkThhe paRhange. Us ton baad tu oh dass
          jehda tainu chahida ae, oh nahi jehda tainu lagda ae
          main seh sakdi haan.
